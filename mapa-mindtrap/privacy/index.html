Privacy Policy for MaPa MindTrap

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · Last updated: 3 November 2026

MaPa MindTrap is an offline brain-puzzle game for Android published by MaPaInfinity. This policy describes what information the game uses, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Information stored on your device

To provide gameplay and remember your preferences, MaPa MindTrap stores game information on your device, which may include:

  • scores, personal bests, completed runs, daily challenge results, streaks, and progress history;
  • game choices and settings, including theme, sound, haptics, and accessibility-related preferences; and
  • locally generated puzzle state or other information necessary to operate the game.

This information is stored in application-private device storage, including a local SQLite database. MaPa MindTrap does not operate a user account service or cloud synchronization service. Gameplay does not require you to provide your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Data collection and sharing

The current version of MaPa MindTrap does not collect or transmit your gameplay records to MaPaInfinity servers. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or remote crash-reporting services. MaPaInfinity does not sell your gameplay information.

If you choose to share a game result, MaPa MindTrap invokes Android's share functionality. The content you select to share is then handled by the app or service you choose, under that service's own privacy practices. Sharing is optional and initiated by you.

If you choose to open a publisher link or contact support through another app, that action may involve your browser, email provider, or the destination service. MaPa MindTrap does not automatically upload your scores or progress as part of those actions.

3. Permissions and internet access

Core gameplay works offline. The current production Android application does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or SMS messages, and does not require a runtime internet connection for play.

Android system functionality, such as sharing, opening a link, or sending an email through another application, may use services outside MaPa MindTrap if you choose to use them.

4. Data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings remain in the app's private device storage until removed by you or by the Android operating system. You can remove locally stored MaPa MindTrap data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Clearing the app's storage also removes locally saved progress and preferences.

Android automatic app-data backup is disabled in the current application configuration. Because MaPaInfinity does not maintain a server-side copy of your game progress, we cannot remotely recover or delete that local data.

Information you previously shared through another application is controlled by the destination application or service, and is not deleted by uninstalling MaPa MindTrap.

5. Children and families

MaPa MindTrap does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer-operated online service. The game's intended audience is declared separately in its Google Play listing. Parents and guardians should review applicable device settings and any third-party app selected for sharing.

6. Data security

Game information is held in Android application-private storage. MaPa MindTrap disables Android's automatic app-data backup and does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization. Local storage is not described here as encrypted by the game. As with any device-stored information, physical device security and Android settings help protect access.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if MaPa MindTrap's features, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date on this page will reflect revisions. Material changes to data practices should be described here before or when the relevant game update is released.

8. Contact

If you have a question about this policy or the game's privacy practices, contact MaPaInfinity at mapauniverse@gmail.com.
